About MeltPet

Evidence-backed pet guides, calculators, and tools — built by researchers who care about getting the facts right.

🐾 About MeltPet

The name MeltPet comes from a simple idea: every person deserves to find the companion that melts their heart. "Melt" your doubts about pet ownership, find your "Pet" perfect match.

MeltPet was founded in 2026 by a team with 5+ years of combined experience in the pet food industry — spanning ingredient sourcing, nutritional formulation, and product development. We've seen what goes into the bag and what stays out. We've watched brands reformulate recipes to cut costs without changing the label. We've answered thousands of confused questions from owners standing in the kibble aisle, overwhelmed by marketing claims and contradictory advice.

Our research team includes contributors with backgrounds in clinical research — experience that taught us how to read a study, how to spot methodology problems, and how to tell the difference between a real finding and a press release. That lens shapes every guide on this site. We don't cite studies we haven't read in full. We don't make claims we can't back up with actual data or veterinary consensus.

🧪 Our Editorial Standards

Every article on MeltPet goes through a structured review process before publication:

  1. Source Verification: All health and behavioral claims are cross-checked against peer-reviewed veterinary journals, AVMA guidelines, and published ethology research.
  2. Data Freshness: Cost estimates and product recommendations are reviewed and updated quarterly against current market prices and availability.
  3. Transparency: We clearly label any affiliate relationships. Product recommendations are based on ingredients, formulation quality, and real-world outcomes — never on commission rates.

Most pet content on the web falls into one of three buckets: AI-generated filler that regurgitates the same 200 words across a hundred sites; sponsor-influenced recommendations where the "best" product is the one paying the highest commission; or outdated forum threads with conflicting anecdotes from strangers. MeltPet is our answer to all three: evidence-backed, industry-informed, and honestly written.

We don't accept sponsor payments from pet food or product brands. Affiliate commissions help keep the site running, but they don't determine what gets recommended. If we wouldn't buy it for our own animals, it doesn't go on the page.

What You'll Find Here

Breed Finder. An interactive quiz that matches your lifestyle to the right dog or cat breed. No signup, no email — just honest recommendations backed by breed-specific data.

Pet Food & Nutrition Guides. Ingredient breakdowns, brand transparency comparisons, diet-type pros and cons (kibble vs raw vs fresh vs freeze-dried), and what years in the industry taught us that labels don't tell you.

Cost Calculators. Real numbers on what a pet actually costs — monthly, yearly, and across their lifetime. Food, vet, insurance, and the hidden costs most guides ignore.

Behavior Explainers. Research-backed answers to the questions every pet owner asks: Why does my cat knead me? Why does my dog tilt its head? Every answer cites specific studies you can look up yourself.

Breed Deep Dives. Comprehensive guides covering personality, costs, health risks, exercise needs, and living situation fit — for every popular breed, written with the unvarnished truth most breed guides skip.
