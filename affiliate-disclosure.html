Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The Short Version

MeltPet is free to read because some of the links on it pay us a commission. If you click one of those links and buy something, the retailer or insurer may pay us a referral fee. You never pay more because of it, and the fee never changes what we recommend.

We have no sponsorships, no paid placements, and no brand has ever paid us to appear in a guide, a comparison table, or a calculator. If that ever changes, it will be stated in plain language at the top of the page concerned, not buried here.

What Compensation Does Not Buy

This is the part that matters, so it is worth being specific rather than reassuring.

  • It does not buy a place in a comparison. Our litter comparison includes options that pay us nothing and options that pay us less than the winner. The ranking follows absorbency, dust, tracking and cost per month.
  • It does not buy a favourable number. Calculator outputs are computed from published data and shown whatever they come out to. If a sponsored brand looked expensive in one of our cost tools, it would still look expensive.
  • It does not buy silence. Where the evidence on a product category is weak, we say so. Where an insurance structure has a real drawback, such as a per-condition deductible instead of an annual one, we describe it.
  • It does not buy a recommendation at all. Some pages recommend nothing. Several of our guides conclude that the honest answer is to spend less, or to do nothing, and they stay that way.

How You Can Tell

Affiliate links on MeltPet carry the attributes rel="nofollow sponsored". That markup exists for search engines, but it also means you can check any link before you click it: in most browsers, right-click the link and inspect it, and the sponsored value will be visible in the HTML.

Outbound links to sources, studies and regulators are never affiliate links. A citation to a journal article or a veterinary school pays us nothing, and we do not add tracking to them.

Prices and Figures Change

We do not publish live prices, because a number scraped into a page is wrong within weeks and there is no way for a reader to tell which numbers are stale. Where a page discusses cost, the figure is either a dated estimate with its basis stated, or a computed range you can re-run yourself with current inputs.

Always confirm the current price, premium or coverage term on the seller's own page before you buy. Nothing on MeltPet is a quote, an offer, or a substitute for the policy documents.

If Something Looks Wrong

If you find a page where the recommendation appears to follow the commission rather than the evidence, tell us and we will look at it. That is a real failure mode in this industry and we would rather hear about it than not.
