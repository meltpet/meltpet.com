Contact MeltPet

One inbox, read by the person who wrote the page.

The Inbox

support@meltpet.com — that is the only address. There is no ticket system, no contact form, and no second channel where a message might get answered faster. Opening the link should bring up your mail app; if it does not, the address above can be copied straight into whatever you use.

MeltPet is written by one editor. The same person reads this inbox, which is why a message containing a specific page and a specific claim gets answered while "I love your site!" usually does not. Nothing here is triaged by staff — every message is read by a human who can act on it.

What Gets Fixed Fastest

A wrong number. Send the page URL, the exact sentence or figure, and the source you are checking it against — a paper, a label, a manufacturer's guaranteed analysis, a vet textbook. If you are right, the page changes and its update date changes with it. Describing a disagreement with a source is more useful than stating the correction, because it lets us decide whether the original reading was wrong or the source has since changed.

A calculator that returns something impossible. Tell us the inputs you entered and the output you saw. Our calculators only do arithmetic on numbers that appear in the article text, so a broken output usually means either a bad assumption we can trace or a script bug we can reproduce. Save the inputs exactly as typed.

Something wrong in the Pin Gallery. A comment, an image, or a board that should not be there. Reports about copyright or about a person appearing in an image are handled first and asked about second — the item comes down before we start the back-and-forth.

The full set of rules we hold ourselves to, including where each kind of number comes from, is on the Editorial Standards page. If you are writing to dispute a figure, that page will tell you which category it was filed under and what was combined to produce it.

What We Do Not Accept

MeltPet does not sell links, publish guest posts, or swap placements. Messages offering a "DR 60 guest post", a "niche edit", a "contextual link insertion", or a paid roundup are declined, and the answer will not change on the second or third ask.

The commercial side of this site is Amazon Associates links, disclosed at the top of every page that carries one — see the Affiliate Disclosure. No brand has paid for a mention, a ranking, or a rewrite, and none is currently able to: there is no rate card to ask for.

This is not a moral position so much as a maintenance one. The value of this site is that a number on it can be traced. A paid placement breaks that in a way no disclosure line repairs.

Press, Data, and Research Requests

Journalists and researchers are welcome to ask for the working behind any figure here — a monthly cost, a feeding ratio, a breed sample size, the assumptions inside a calculator. Say what you need and when you need it. When the underlying working exists, it gets sent in a form you can check; when a number was an estimate rather than a measurement, you will be told that plainly so it is not quoted as if it were measured.

Please say whether you are on deadline. A request with a date attached moves to the top of the queue; a request without one may sit for a week.

Rights and Permissions

Short quotes are fine. You may quote a sentence or a paragraph with a link back to the page it came from. No permission needed, no fee.

Republishing a whole article, chart, or calculator is not. That includes translation, "lightly edited" reprints, and automated scraping for a content farm. The text, the illustrations, and the logic inside the tools are covered by copyright. If you want to republish something in full, ask first and say where it will appear.

Automated collection. The robots.txt file states what is and is not welcome. AI training crawlers are asked to stay out; search and answer engines that send a reader to the page are welcome, and the llms.txt file exists so they can navigate the site without guessing.

Privacy and Data Requests

There are no accounts on MeltPet, so there is no profile to download or delete. What can exist is a gallery comment you posted and an anonymous hashed identifier used to stop spam — both are described in the Privacy Policy, and cookies are itemised separately in the Cookie Policy.

If you want a comment removed, email with "gallery" in the subject and include the pin it was posted on. If it is your own comment, say so; there is no login that could prove it, so removal is done on request rather than on verification.

Response Times, Honestly

Expect days, not hours. A correction sent on a Tuesday may be fixed the following week, because checking a claim properly means opening the source rather than accepting the correction. Silence is not a refusal; if a week passes with no reply, send it again with the same subject line — the thread makes it obvious that it is a second attempt rather than a new request.

What will not happen: automated replies, a support bot, or a promise of a 24-hour turnaround. There is one person, and the honest range is one to seven days.
