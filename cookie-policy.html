Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Three Sentences, Then the Detail

MeltPet sets no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking cookies. It sets one analytics cookie, and only after you press Accept on the banner. Everything else the site remembers about you is stored in your own browser and never transmitted.

If you want the short version of your options: press Decline and the site keeps working exactly as it does now, minus the cookie. The Privacy Policy covers the wider question of what data exists; this page covers only what is stored on your device.

How the Banner Works

The consent banner appears on your first visit and stores your answer in local storage under meltpet-cookie-consent. Your choice is tagged with a version number. If the categories of cookie ever change enough to matter, that version is incremented and the banner asks again — it does not silently inherit an old Accept for a new kind of tracking.

Accepting grants analytics storage, which is what allows the _ga cookies to exist. Declining does not merely skip them: it also deletes any _ga, _gid or _gat cookie that is already present. If you accept and later clear your browser data, the banner will ask once more, because your answer lived in that storage.

What Happens If You Decline

Declining is honest but not absolute, and it is worth being precise about the difference. Google Analytics 4 still loads in a restricted mode: with analytics storage denied, no cookies are written and no identifier persists between pages, but an anonymised, cookieless signal can still be sent so that aggregate traffic volume is not wildly undercounted. What that signal cannot do is recognise you on your next visit, follow you to another site, or be joined to a profile.

If you want to go further than the banner, a content blocker or a browser set to clear storage on exit will stop both the cookie and the signal. Nothing on this site breaks when you do that — the calculators run locally and have no dependency on analytics.

Regions Are Treated Differently

Visitors in the EU, EEA and UK are served with analytics storage denied by default, before any banner interaction. That means the cookieless state is the starting condition rather than an opt-out, which is the arrangement GDPR and the ePrivacy rules actually ask for. Visitors elsewhere start with analytics storage granted and can revoke it by declining or by clearing storage.

Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation are denied for every visitor in every region, because this site runs no advertising. Those switches exist in the implementation so that they cannot be turned on by accident.

What Is Not Here

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies — there is no ad network on this site.
  • No Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest or LinkedIn pixels.
  • No session recording, heatmap, or A/B testing script.
  • No cross-site tracking of any kind, and no data sold or shared with a data broker.
  • No cookie wall: declining costs you nothing and blocks no content.

Amazon links on this site do not set a cookie on our domain. Clicking one takes you to Amazon, where Amazon's own cookies and privacy policy apply from that point on.

Changing Your Mind

To withdraw consent, clear site data for meltpet.com in your browser settings. The banner will reappear on your next visit and you can decline. To check what is currently stored, your browser's developer tools show cookies and local storage for this domain in one place — the list should match the table above and nothing more.

If you believe something is being stored that this page does not describe, that is a bug worth reporting through Contact, and it will be treated as one.

If Advertising Arrives

This site does not run ads. If that changes, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated before any advertising cookie is set, the consent banner will be re-prompted with the new categories, and visitors in regions that require opt-in will be asked before anything loads. A silent switch to an ad network is the one thing this page is written to rule out.
