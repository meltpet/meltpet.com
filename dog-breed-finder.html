🐶 Questions Dog People Actually Ask

Which dog breed is best for first-time owners?

Golden Retrievers and Labrador Retrievers top the list —they're eager to please, forgiving of training mistakes, and naturally good with families. Cavalier King Charles Spaniels are another excellent choice for apartment dwellers. The common thread: breeds that were developed to work with humans (not independently) tend to be easier for beginners.

What's the best dog for an apartment?

Size isn't the deciding factor —energy level is. French Bulldogs, Cavaliers, and surprisingly, Greyhounds (they're couch potatoes who need one short sprint per day) all thrive in apartments. A small but high-energy Jack Russell Terrier will be far more destructive in an apartment than a calm Great Dane.

Are mixed breeds better than purebreds?

Mixed breeds often benefit from hybrid vigor —lower risk of breed-specific genetic conditions. But purebred dogs have predictable traits: you know a Golden Retriever will be friendly, and a Border Collie will herd your kids. If you're open to either, we always recommend visiting your local shelter. Tell them your lifestyle parameters (the same ones in this quiz) and they'll introduce you to dogs that fit.

How accurate is the dog breed finder?

Our recommendations are evidence-based, not guaranteed. Breed temperament data gives strong statistical probabilities —Golden Retrievers are highly likely to be good with children, Border Collies need significant mental stimulation —but individual dogs vary based on genetics, socialization, and training. Use the breed finder as your starting point, then meet the breed in person before deciding.
