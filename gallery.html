What this board is for

Most pet advice is text, and text is slow. A pin gets the useful part in front of you in one second: the breed's real exercise need, which foods are dangerous, how much to feed. The picture is the hook — the numbers behind it live in the tool it links to.

Every pin here points at a page that does the actual work. Breed boards link to the full breed guides and to the weight calculator. Food safety pins link to the toxic food checker. Feeding pins link to the raw feeding calculator and calorie calculator. Cost pins link to the monthly cost calculator.

Comments are open without an account. They are reviewed before they appear, so anything that does show up was read by a human first — that is also why an obviously spammy comment will simply never surface. Disagree with a number on a pin? Say so in the comments and it gets checked against the source.
