Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Our Approach

MeltPet is a static website. All tools (Breed Finder, cost calculators, Toxic Food Checker) run entirely in your browser —no personal data is sent to any server. We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how many people visit and which pages are useful. GA4 uses minimal first-party cookies (_ga) for basic traffic measurement. IP addresses are anonymized and no personally identifiable information is collected.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or personal identification
  • Browsing history across sites
  • Exact location data
  • Device fingerprints

GA4 shows us aggregated data —total page views, which countries visitors come from (city-level is not available), and which pages are most read. We cannot identify individual visitors from this data.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics 4 (GA4). We use GA4 for anonymous traffic measurement. GA4 sets minimal first-party cookies (_ga) that do not store personal data. IP addresses are anonymized. You can opt out by declining cookies when the consent banner appears, or by using a browser extension. Learn more at Google's Analytics privacy page.

Cloudflare Pages. MeltPet is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may collect anonymized server logs for security and performance purposes. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at cloudflare.com.

Future Advertising. If MeltPet runs ads in the future (e.g., Google AdSense), this policy will be updated, and visitors will be notified. No advertising networks are currently active.
