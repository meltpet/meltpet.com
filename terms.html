Not Veterinary or Financial Advice

MeltPet publishes educational material about dogs and cats. It is written by an editor who reads primary veterinary and regulatory sources, not by a licensed veterinarian, and it is not a substitute for examination by one. No page on this site establishes a veterinarian-client-patient relationship, and no response to an email does either.

Health guides describe what a symptom can mean and when it warrants same-day attention. That is triage information. It is deliberately not a diagnosis, and it should not be used to decide against seeking care. If your animal is struggling to breathe, has swallowed something toxic, cannot urinate, or is collapsing, the correct action is a phone call to a veterinarian or an emergency clinic — not another paragraph of reading.

Cost figures are retail ranges sampled from public listings at the time a page was updated, not quotes, and not financial advice. What you will actually spend depends on where you live, what your animal needs, and prices that move after we last looked.

What the Calculators Are

Every calculator on MeltPet does arithmetic on numbers that also appear in the article text, so a result can be traced back to a stated input. Results are presented as ranges wherever the underlying data is a range.

A calculator result is an estimate produced from the inputs you entered. It is not a quote, a guarantee, a diagnosis, or a substitute for a professional assessment. Feeding amounts, exercise targets, and cost projections all shift with the individual animal and the household, and no formula on this site knows your animal. Use the output as a starting point and check it against a veterinarian for anything health-related.

If a tool returns something implausible for sane inputs, that is a bug and we want to hear about it — see Contact.
